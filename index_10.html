Our Approach

The difference between
reporting and planning.

Most firms meet you once a year to record what already happened. ITXRE works alongside you all year so every major decision is made with its tax consequences in view.

“The best tax outcome is decided long before April.”
  1. I

    Discovery

    A private conversation to understand your income, holdings, goals, and the advisors already on your team.

  2. II

    Strategy

    A written plan with projections, priorities, and a calendar of decisions for the year ahead.

  3. III

    Execution

    Quarterly check-ins, estimated payments, and coordination with your attorney and wealth manager.

  4. IV

    Preparation

    Precise, reviewed returns that reflect the strategy, delivered through a secure client portal.
